Yellow Rose Showcase returns to Cullen Park in Houston on March 6–7, 2027 — same tournament, same place.

It is an automatic CRC qualifier for Men's Premiere, Men's D1-AA, Men's D2, and Men's D3. Women's rugby joins the weekend in 2027. Ultimate frisbee brackets run separately that weekend and are not part of this rugby referee application.

Pōpolo is recruiting match officials for this event. Apply below if you want to be considered for the crew. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis — we will follow up by email.

## What we know now

- **When:** March 6–7, 2027
- **Where:** Cullen Park, Houston, TX
- **Format:** Rugby 7s
- **CRC automatic qualifier:** Men's Premiere, Men's D1-AA, Men's D2, and Men's D3
- **New in 2027:** Women's rugby
- **Also on site:** Men's and women's ultimate frisbee (separate event)

Last year, nine Yellow Rose programs competed at CRC Nationals, including Denver (D3 runner-up), Texas A&M, Sam Houston State, Indiana, Montana State, Utah State, New Mexico Tech, St. Edward's, and Harvard.

Lodging and crew logistics will land here as they lock in. You can still apply now so we have you on the list.
