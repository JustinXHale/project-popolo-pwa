## What it is

CMO Align is Project Pōpolo’s program for referee coaches (CMOs). It exists because coaches watching the same community referee too often see different things, coach different things, and assign different grades.

The goal is not identical reports. The goal is a shared evaluation standard — so when a CMO is assigned a referee this weekend, what they notice, what they prioritize, and how they grade would hold up against a common bar.

## How it works

A small core panel of coaches reviews the same clip, agrees the coaching priorities, and agrees the grade. That is how the standard is set.

Everyone else works on their own time. Participating CMOs log in, watch community-referee footage, submit a structured coaching report and grade, then see how they compare to the panel. They do not see the standard until they have submitted. Matching a number is not enough — the useful test is whether they would coach the same two or three things.

Clips stay in the program so coaches in different time zones can work more than one object without sitting on a 40-person call.

## Who it is for

College CMOs and other appointed referee coaches in the Pōpolo circle. Coaches sign in with the same account as the rest of the site. If you want to take part, [contact us](/contact).

CMO Align sits in the [Align](/about/schedule) step of the 12-month cycle — camps, film, and coaching reports.
