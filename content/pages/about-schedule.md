## 12-month cycle

1. **Identify** — talent ID with partners and local societies
2. **Align** — camps, film, coaching reports, and [CMO Align](/about/align)
3. **Appoint** — matches logged by level, format, and role
4. **Advocate** — public profiles so officials are not passed over
5. **Review** — evaluations stay on the record, good and bad

Live dates and Round Table notes are on [Calendar & minutes](/about/calendar). Replace this outline when the cycle changes.
