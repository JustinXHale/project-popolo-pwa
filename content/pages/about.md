## Mission

Project Pōpolo exists to grow Black and Polynesian match officials in the USA Rugby pathways. We mentor, advocate, and put faces and match records in front of the people who make appointments.

## Program

- A 12-month cycle of guidance and support
- Advocacy inside local referee organizations
- Camps, trips, and high-performance exposure
- Transparent match logs and evaluations
- [CMO Align](/about/align) — a shared standard for referee coaches

We work as a group. The [Round Table](/about#round-table) is the working circle — operations, performance, culture, and advocacy around one table. Dates and notes are on [Calendar & minutes](/about/calendar).
