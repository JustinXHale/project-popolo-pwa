Project Pōpolo is dedicated to fostering opportunities for Black and Polynesian rugby referees within the USA Rugby Pathways framework. Our initiative is a 12-month program of guidance, mentorship, and advocacy inside local referee organizations.

The goal is representation in the National Referee Development Group and High-Performance Group — and a public record of the work, so officials are seen.
