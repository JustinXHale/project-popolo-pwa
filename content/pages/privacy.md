Project Pōpolo publishes referee names, photos that officials upload, match logs, and evaluation PDFs. We do **not** publish phone numbers, emails, or street addresses.

Account data lives in Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage). You can delete your account from the portal; that removes your login and associated records.

Contact and tournament-request forms store organizer contact details for admins only.
